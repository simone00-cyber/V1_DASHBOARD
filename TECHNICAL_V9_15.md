# Technical Analysis v9.15

- Support and resistance are rendered only as high-visibility green/red zones.
- No text or level labels are drawn over the candlestick chart.
- The nearest support and resistance zones are more opaque than secondary zones.
- Volume is displayed in a dedicated panel between price and RSI when available.
- Volume bars are green/red according to the candle direction.
- Candles, volume and RSI share the same interactive time axis and move together during pan and zoom.

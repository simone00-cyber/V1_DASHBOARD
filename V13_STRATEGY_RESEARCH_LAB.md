# v13.0 — Research & Backtesting Lab

The Strategy Lab now contains two independent engines:

- Technical Strategy Lab: visual rule builder, long/short execution, explicit costs/slippage, stop loss, take profit, trailing stop, time exit, complete metrics, professional trade ledger and inspector, parameter sensitivity/grid search, walk-forward testing, Monte Carlo trade resampling, and reproducible JSON/text reports.
- Cyclical Strategy Lab: the existing documented public cyclical matrix backtest, research diagnostics, audit trail and methodology registry. No undisclosed proprietary Caruso formula is introduced.

Every technical result stores the exact strategy specification and execution assumptions used by the engine.

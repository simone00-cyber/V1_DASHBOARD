# V13.2 Guided Strategy Builder

The Technical Strategy Lab now uses a five-step guided workflow:

1. Instrument
2. Entry
3. Exit
4. Risk & Execution
5. Review & Run

The builder is ticker-based and does not ask for a market universe. Each condition remains fully parameterised, including indicator, periods, multiplier, comparison operator, constant or indicator comparison, and persistence. Logical operators are presented in plain language as "all conditions" or "at least one condition". Short rules remain separately configurable.

The exact machine-readable specification continues to be saved in the backtest result and research report.

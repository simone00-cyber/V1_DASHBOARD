# Market Screener v9.5

## Methodology Screener

- User selects only the index universe.
- No history selector.
- No Opportunity Score or weighted Cyclical Score.
- Matrix Action and Reward/Risk Rating are generated from the implemented public quarterly/monthly/weekly matrix.
- Display order uses Matrix Action, published Rating and transparent cyclical tie-breakers.

## Sector Ranking

- User selects 1 day, 1 week, 1 month or 1 year.
- Sectors are ranked by equal-weight average adjusted-price return of their analysed constituents.

## Top & Flop

- User selects the same performance windows and optionally a sector.
- Securities are ranked only by adjusted-price return.

## Data conventions

- Adjusted prices neutralise dividends and splits.
- Internal history remains fixed at five years to calculate quarterly, monthly and weekly Composite Momentum.

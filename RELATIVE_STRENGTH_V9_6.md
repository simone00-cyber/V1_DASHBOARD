# Relative Strength v9.6

The Market Screener now includes a dedicated **RELATIVE STRENGTH** tab.

## Benchmarks

- NASDAQ 100: `^NDX`
- S&P 500: `^GSPC`
- FTSE MIB: `FTSEMIB.MI`
- DAX 40: `^GDAXI`

## Definition

Relative Strength is calculated as the security adjusted-price return minus the benchmark adjusted-price return over the selected horizon. The result is expressed in percentage points.

Available horizons: 1 week, 1 month, 3 months, 6 months and 1 year.

The 1-year RS Ratio is the stock/benchmark price ratio normalised to 100 at the beginning of the 252-session window.

Relative Strength is a framework comparison tool. It does not change or replace the public cyclical Matrix signals and ratings.

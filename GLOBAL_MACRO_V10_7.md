# Global Macro v10.7

## Yield curve workflow

The rates section now follows a top-down workflow:

1. Headline US Treasury and euro-area AAA yield curves are displayed first with stable default maturities.
2. Maturity selectors appear immediately before the historical charts they control.
3. Historical US and euro yield charts update independently from those selectors.

The top curves are no longer changed by the history filters, preserving a consistent at-a-glance macro view.

# Relative Strength Lab v9.7

The Market Screener now includes an interactive Relative Strength Lab:

- predefined or custom Yahoo Finance benchmark;
- index constituents and custom comparison tickers;
- optional equal-weight sector composites;
- normalized performance chart (common start = 100);
- relative-strength ratio chart;
- side leader board with status, sector, Matrix Action and excess-return filters;
- return, volatility, correlation, beta, tracking error and information ratio;
- monthly leader-rotation monitor;
- multi-horizon relative-strength heatmap;
- CSV export.

Relative Strength is kept separate from the public cyclical Matrix. It is a framework comparison tool and does not create or modify BUY, TAKE PROFIT or SELL SHORT signals.

# Technical Analysis Lab v9.8

A single page combining an index-level technical screener and a detailed security lab.

Features:
- clustered swing support/resistance zones;
- near-support, near-resistance, breakout and breakdown filters;
- configurable moving averages and crossover detection;
- configurable RSI with overbought/oversold and swing-based divergence candidates;
- potential double top/bottom, triangles, pennants, flags and cup-and-handle candidates;
- one-page drill-down with candlestick, levels, moving averages, RSI and descriptive events;
- no BUY/SELL generation and no modification of the cyclical methodology.

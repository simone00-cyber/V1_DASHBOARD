# Technical Analysis v9.13

## Pattern Engine

The technical module now recognises transparent heuristic candidates for:

- head and shoulders / inverse head and shoulders;
- double and triple tops/bottoms;
- ascending, descending and symmetrical triangles;
- rising and falling wedges;
- rectangles, ascending/descending channels and broadening formations;
- bullish/bearish flags;
- cup and handle;
- rounded tops/bottoms.

Every candidate includes geometry anchors, category, directional interpretation, confidence (0-100) and lifecycle state: DEVELOPING, CONFIRMED or RETESTED. These are descriptive research outputs, never BUY/SELL signals.

## Dynamic levels

Support and resistance zones retain their origin date, touches, strength and lifecycle. A broken resistance may become support only after a successful retest; a broken support may become resistance under the symmetric rule. Broken or failed zones are shown with dashed borders.

## Visual improvements

- nearest support is highlighted in bright green;
- nearest resistance is highlighted in bright red;
- secondary levels remain visible with lower opacity;
- level labels include price, lifecycle role and strength;
- pattern charts retain the full available history and expose anchors, boundaries, confidence and lifecycle;
- price and RSI remain synchronised and interactive.

## Screener loading state

The Market Screener now replaces the previous page with a staged loading panel while data, Composite Momentum, Matrix output and rankings are prepared.
